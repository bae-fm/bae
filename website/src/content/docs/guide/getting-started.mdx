bae is a home for the albums you own. You build the library on your desktop, and it follows you to every device: synced through cloud storage you already have, encrypted so only your devices can read it.

The desktop apps (macOS, Windows) are the full tool: they create libraries, import music, and edit metadata. The phone apps (iOS, Android) are players: they join a library your desktop created, then browse, download, and play.

## Create a library

Open bae on your desktop. With no library on the machine, the welcome screen offers three choices:

- **Create a new library**, if this is your first device.
- **Join a library**, if another device already has one and its owner will approve you. See [Devices and members](/guide/devices).
- **Restore from cloud**, if this is your own library and you have its recovery code. See [Devices and members](/guide/devices#recovery-codes).

Create one. A library starts local: everything lives on this machine, nothing needs an account, and no cloud is involved until you connect one.

## Import your first album

1. Switch to the **Import** section in the title bar.
2. Add a folder of music. You can pick it, drag it onto the window, or press <kbd>⌘I</kbd>.
3. bae scans the folder and lists the releases it finds. Select one.
4. bae identifies it against MusicBrainz and Discogs and shows the match. Confirm the pressing, pick a cover, adjust any details.
5. Click **Import**.

Your files are not moved, copied, or rewritten. bae catalogs them where they are. The full flow, including what to do when identification needs help, is in [Importing](/guide/importing).

## Play it

Back in the **Library** section, the album is in the grid. Click it, press play. Playback uses your original files, at their original quality. [Playback](/guide/playback) covers the queue, shuffle and repeat, and the details bae preserves: CD pregaps, vinyl side breaks, gapless albums.

## Take it everywhere

When you want the library on more than one device, connect a cloud home: your own Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud, or any S3-compatible bucket. Everything bae puts there is encrypted before upload; the provider stores it but cannot read it.

- [Sync](/guide/sync): connect a cloud home and choose what it stores.
- [Devices and members](/guide/devices): add your phone or another desktop.
- [Storage and offline](/guide/storage): keep music on the device, or stream it from your cloud and pin what you need offline.

## Where things live

Each library keeps its database and settings under `~/.bae/libraries/` on desktop. Your audio stays wherever you imported it from, unless you ask bae to manage it in your cloud. Keys and credentials live in the operating system's keyring, not in files.
