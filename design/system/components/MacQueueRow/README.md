# MacQueueRow

A queued track, drawn as a playlist page's row (`MacListRow`): the 40pt cover beside the title over the artists, with no album, and the length at the end. `hover`: the row under the pointer tints and Remove from queue joins its end, after the length.

**In the code:** macOS: `QueueItemRow` (`Views/Playback/Queue/QueueItemRow.swift`).
