# MacPlaylistPage

A playlist's page inside the side pane. Clicking a playlist in the pane's Playlists tab opens its page there, and the window keeps the library's view. While it is open, the pane's top shows the round back button (Back to Playlists) where the + was, which returns to the list (`MacQueuePanel` with `back`). The page's header is laid out as the album page's: the playlist's picture (103pt) beside a column of its name, up to two lines; its owner, on a line of their own; the facts line, track count and length; then Play and the ⋯ (Playlist options), on a control's fill as the album page's, under the text at its start. The ⋯ menu offers Shuffle to everyone, then the owner's `options` (Edit playlist…, Delete playlist…). On the person's own playlist the facts line ends in who sees it (`sharing`), as a lock for Only me or two people for Everyone, which screen readers name; a playlist someone else shared shows nothing about sharing, since sharing is how they see it. The tracks follow as its children, `MacListRow`s numbered from 1, each with its 40pt cover (`cover`) and its credit line under the title, no album, whose numbers hang in the gutter, right-aligned in a 20pt column 8pt from the pane's edge, left of the picture's edge, so each cover lines up under the picture. The pane's tabs stay switchable while a playlist is open, and the Playlists tab shows the open playlist again when the person comes back to it. Put it as the child of a `MacQueuePanel` on the Playlists tab.

The page draws its header with `MacPlaylistHeader`, which takes the same props.

**In the code:** macOS: `PlaylistDetailPane` (`Views/Library/Playlists/PlaylistDetailPane.swift`) draws the page's tracks, and a header with the buttons at its end and who sees it in words, as the detail of the Playlists view's split, in the window; the side pane holds no page yet.
