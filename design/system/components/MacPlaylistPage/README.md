# MacPlaylistPage

A playlist's page inside the side pane. Clicking a playlist in the pane's Playlists tab opens its page there, and the window keeps the library's view. While it is open, the pane's top shows the round back button (Back to Playlists) where the + was, which returns to the list (`MacQueuePanel` with `back`). The page starts with the app's header: the playlist's picture (72pt) beside its name, up to two lines, over one line of owner, track count and length and who sees it. Play, Shuffle and the owner's ⋯ (`options`, named Playlist options: Edit playlist…, Delete playlist…) end the header in the window; the pane's 420pt leaves the name and that line too little room beside them, so they sit in a row under it, at its right end as in the window. The tracks follow as its children, `MacListRow`s numbered from 1. The pane's tabs stay switchable while a playlist is open, and the Playlists tab shows the open playlist again when the person comes back to it. Put it as the child of a `MacQueuePanel` on the Playlists tab.

**In the code:** macOS: `PlaylistDetailPane` (`Views/Library/Playlists/PlaylistDetailPane.swift`) draws the same header and tracks as the detail of the Playlists view's split, in the window; the side pane holds no page yet.
