# MacSheet

A panel rounded on every corner, just under the title bar, with its title bold at the top of the content; the window behind isn't dimmed. Ends with a `MacSheetFooter`. Cancel goes back one screen, and closes the sheet on the first one. No Back buttons.

`message` is the line under the title, in secondary text, as an alert's message: what the sheet asks for or reports. It's never drawn by hand in the body.

**In the code:** macOS: SwiftUI's `.sheet`.
