# MacAlbumPanel

The album opened in the grid, in a panel under its row: cover (opens the gallery), title, artists · year, the release picker when there are several, the pressing and labels, why this Mac can't play it when it can't, Play and the album's menu, then the tracks. Close in the corner.

**In the code:** macOS: `AlbumExpansionContent` (`Views/Library/AlbumDetail/AlbumExpansionContent.swift`) inside `AlbumDetailView`.
