# AndroidActivity

The Activity sheet's list, opened from the library's top bar: the title, then Needs Attention, each problem a quiet row (no fill, the glyph in its tone) with a line between rows: (problems the caller passes in as children, each an `AndroidNotice`, bae couldn't read sync's status, with no button, and sync's own with Open Sync Settings, which closes the sheet and opens sync settings; nothing retries in place), then the transfers as on macOS, each its words, how far it has got (releases done of the batch, "1 of 2 releases", or what holds the queue back: Paused, Waiting for network, and for uploads also Waiting for Wi-Fi and Retrying at 6:40 PM; the bar by bytes) and a bar, syncing with a moving bar (the pinning line opens the Pinning screen), or up to date. The problems' words start past their icons, which stand in a column of their own at the edge; the Needs Attention label sits at the edge with the title, and the transfers sit under a Transfers label of their own, as on macOS; the title and the transfers are their own groups and start at the sheet's edge, since an indent never runs across groups. A row has no Details: tapping it opens a dialog with the explanation and the error's causes (`AndroidDialog` holding `AndroidErrorDetails`). Each problem has an × that dismisses it from the sheet; that doesn't fix it, and it still shows where it's fixed (such as Settings → Sync). A problem shows here and nowhere else on the screen; the top bar's button shows the warning glyph while a problem that hasn't been dismissed is listed; once every problem is dismissed, it goes back to its usual look. The Pinning line ends in a tonal Pause button, Resume (a play symbol) only while the person has paused pinning (`downloadsWait` is Paused), not while the network holds it; Uploading has none, since a phone never uploads release files. While a queue is held (by any of those words) and nothing moves, the button shows a pause symbol, so the hold can be seen and undone from here. Put it in an `AndroidSheet`.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidActivity` (`ui/sync/Activity.kt`) in `ActivityButton`'s sheet, its transfer lines in `ui/sync/ActivityTransfers.kt`.
