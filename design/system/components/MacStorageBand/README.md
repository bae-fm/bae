# MacStorageBand

A release's storage in its Storage sheet, in the order `StorageStatusBand` draws it: one line, the release's state at rest (Local, Pinned, In cloud) and beside it at most one pill for a change on its way, in `MacStorageChip`'s words: Queued to upload or Uploading, Queued to pin or Pinning, Queued to make local or Making local…, the moving ones filling with `progress`. A release a move to the cloud keeps here reads Pinning. A failed pin or make-local is a danger notice under the line, Couldn't pin or Couldn't make local, with Details. An upload held by a missing original is a warning notice naming the file (`missing`). Then why this Mac can't play it, and the storage actions: none while something moves; Cancel pinning in place of Pin while a pin is queued or keeping a moved release here. Nothing says how many wait ahead or what holds the queue back.

**In the code:** macOS: `StorageStatusBand` (`Views/Library/AlbumDetail/StorageStatusBand.swift`), with `QueuedPinChips` and `UploadWaitChip`.
