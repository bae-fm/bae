# MacStorageBand

A release's storage in its Storage sheet, in the order `StorageStatusBand` draws it: one line, the release's state at rest (Local, Pinned, In cloud) and beside it at most one pill for a change on its way, in `MacStorageChip`'s words: Queued to upload or Uploading, Queued to pin or Pinning, Queued to move to This Mac or Moving to This Mac…, the moving ones filling with `progress`. A failed pin or move to This Mac is a danger notice under the line, Could not pin or Could not move to This Mac, with Details. An upload held by a missing original is a warning notice naming the file (`missing`). Then why this Mac can't play it, and the storage actions: none while something moves; Cancel pinning in place of Pin while a pin is queued. Nothing says how many wait ahead or what holds the queue back.

**In the code:** macOS: `StorageStatusBand` (`Views/Library/AlbumDetail/StorageStatusBand.swift`), its pill drawn with the chips in `Views/Storage/Table/StorageStateLabel.swift`. (`QueuedPinChips` and `UploadWaitChip` do not exist.)
