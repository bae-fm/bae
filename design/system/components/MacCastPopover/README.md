# MacCastPopover

A `MacPopover` that opens upward from the Play on button, its arrow at the middle of its lower edge pointing down at the button, headed "Play on" in a `MacEyebrow` on the rows' inset, with no hairline under it. Under the header are the receivers bae found, each a `MacCastRow` (the one playing, `casting`, saying "Playing" under its name with Disconnect at its right, each receiver once), (`devices`, "Name:cast", "Name:airplay", "Name:upnp"), or "No receivers found" in secondary text while it has found none. While bae connects to a receiver (`connecting`, the receiver's name), that receiver's row shows a spinner and says "Connecting…" under its name. Once the receiver holds the connection for its owner to accept, as an AirPlay receiver does (`ownerDeciding`), the row says "Accept on Kitchen…" until the receiver answers. The popover is `flush`: its content sets its own text colour and pads itself 6px at the top, 4px at the sides and 12px at the bottom.

**In the code:** macOS: `MacCastPopover`, which `CastButton` opens (`Views/Playback/NowPlaying/CastButton.swift`).
