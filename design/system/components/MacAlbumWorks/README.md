# MacAlbumWorks

The works an album's tracks record, in the open album under its tracks: the Works heading (`MacFormHeading`) over one line for each work, with its track's number, the work's title in the accent colour and its composer. `works` holds the lines as "number|title|composer", separated by semicolons.

**In the code:** macOS: `MacAlbumWorks` (`Views/Library/AlbumDetail/MacAlbumWorks.swift`).
