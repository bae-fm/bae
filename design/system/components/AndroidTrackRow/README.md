# AndroidTrackRow

Position, title (and the artist on a compilation), duration, options. The current track's title is accent with a speaker (playing) or play (paused) in place of its number.

The row draws its lead with `AndroidTrackLead`. `work` adds a line under the row naming the work the track records and its composer.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidTrackRow` (`ui/albumdetail/AndroidTrackRow.kt`).
