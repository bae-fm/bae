# StatusGlyph

A tone's glyph in the tone's colour, a ring with its mark inside, the one form every status glyph takes: info `i`, success a check, warning and danger `!` (told apart by colour). Info's glyph draws in the accent. Each other tone's glyph takes the tone's icon token (`danger-icon`, `warning-icon`, `success-icon`), worked out from the tone: in light mode the tone made lighter and more saturated, since a glyph needs less darkness than text; in dark mode the tone itself, danger mixed 65% with secondary grey so it stays calmer than the warning's amber. `inline` sizes it at 1.19 times the text beside it, which the notices use. `muted` draws it in grey (`text-tertiary`), for a failed view's centred notice, where it says only that something went wrong. Neutral and accent have none. `set="mac"` draws the macOS stand-in; `set="ring"` the ring with its solid mark that Android's notices lead with; without either, Android's Material Symbol, outlined. `MacNotice`, `AndroidNotice`, the danger chips and the failed states use it.

**In the code:** macOS: BaeKit's `StatusGlyph` (`StatusTone.swift`). Android: `StatusGlyph` (`ui/components/Status.kt`).

`size` on macOS is the icon size the app sets the SF Symbol at: the ring is 1.017 times that wide and its stroke 0.112 times that thick, as the shots measure the symbol; on Android it is the Material icon's box, whose circle fills 20 of its 24.
