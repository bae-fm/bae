# AndroidSpinner

For a wait with no length and no words of its own, such as a list still filling. With `label`, the words say what is happening, ending in an ellipsis: "Looking for receivers…". A wait whose length is known, or a step of a flow, is an `AndroidProgress`.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidSpinner` (`ui/components/AndroidSpinner.kt`), over Material's `CircularProgressIndicator`.
