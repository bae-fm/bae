# AndroidAlbumHeader

Tops the album page: the 150pt cover (opens the gallery when there is one), then the title, the artist and the album's year, each on its own line, then the selected release on two lines when known. When the person has rated the album, its year line ends in the rating read-only, the number and one star in the accent (`rating`, "1992 · 4 ★"); an unrated album shows nothing there (decision #1219). The edition line (`edition`): the release's own year, left out when it matches the album's year above it, each label with its catalog numbers, then its country in the short form the person's language commonly uses, as in `2001 · Harbor Records · HR-1042 · US`. The copy line (`copy`): the media and details, then the length. Core words both lines, and Mac shows the same words. The release lines draw in the tertiary text colour, a step lighter than the artist and year above them. The children, Play, sit under the release lines, beside the cover, with Play's bottom edge on the cover's. The album's other actions are the items of the ⋮ in the top bar (`AndroidDetailScreen` `menu`).

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidAlbumHeader` in `ui/albumdetail/AlbumDetailScreen.kt`.
