# MacPopover

A popover hanging off what it describes, its arrow pointing up at the control that opened it: the toolbar's Activity button, a library row's More button. It holds something to look at with nothing to decide, and closes on a click outside, with no Done. Place it with `top` and `right` inside its window; the arrow sits 40 from its right edge.

**In the code:** macOS: SwiftUI `.popover`.
