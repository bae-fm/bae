# AndroidPageActions

An album or playlist page's play buttons: Play (filled) and Shuffle, then Play next and Add to queue on a second line, each outlined. `short` draws Play and Shuffle alone, as a playlist page has them.

**In the code:** Android: `AlbumActionButtons` (`ui/albumdetail/AlbumDetailScreen.kt`); the playlist page draws its Play and Shuffle itself in `PlaylistContent` (`ui/library/PlaylistScreen.kt`).
