# AndroidSheet

A bottom sheet over its screen, for something secondary to the screen that asks for no decision: the queue, the full player, the cast picker, the Activity sheet, a library's details. `title` names it. It is as tall as what it holds, as Material's modal sheet is, up to near the top of the screen; `top` pins a tall one, such as the queue or the full player, to a height of its own. Swiping down or tapping outside closes it, so it has no Close button. Its drag handle at the top is Material's `BottomSheetDragHandle`, a control TalkBack names "Drag handle". A decision (a confirmation, a choice that has to be made) is an `AndroidDialog`, and a whole task with fields is its own screen. **On iOS** (not built yet) the same content is a sheet too, at its medium height for details and the queue, full height for a task; there a sheet also holds forms, with Cancel and Done at its top. **On macOS** it is a popover for details hanging off what they describe, or a sheet on the window for a task.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidSheet` (`ui/components/AndroidSheet.kt`), with `title` or without, over Material's `ModalBottomSheet`; the queue is held by it, titled. The full player (`AndroidFullPlayerContainer`) calls `ModalBottomSheet` directly: its chevron animates the sheet out with the sheet's own state before dismissing, which `AndroidSheet` keeps to itself.
