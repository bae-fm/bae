# MacWelcomeWindow

The welcome window, 690 by 600 pt unless given: the rounded window with its traffic lights at the top left, no title and no sidebar. The welcome screens, Create, joining and adding this device draw inside it; a `MacAlert` covers it.

**In the code:** macOS: `MacWelcomeWindow` (`Views/Chrome/MainWindowChrome.swift`); `WelcomeWindow` there is the enum holding its size.
