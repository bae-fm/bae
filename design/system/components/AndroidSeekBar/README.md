# AndroidSeekBar

Elapsed, the line with its thumb, the length. `large` is the full player's bar (the player passes it): Material 3's slider: a 16dp track, a bar for the handle and a stop dot at the end. The faint accent is what this device holds of the track; the solid accent is what has played.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidSeekBar` (`ui/playback/AndroidSeekBar.kt`) over the `PlaybackProgressView` Android View and `HeldRangesDrawable` (`ui/playback/`); `AndroidSeekBarContainer` reads the session's position and held ranges for it.
