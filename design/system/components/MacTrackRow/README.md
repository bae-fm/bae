# MacTrackRow

Position, title, duration; the artist under the title on a compilation. The current track's title is accent with a speaker in place of its number; `hover` shows play (or pause) there.

**In the code:** macOS: `MacTrackRow` (`Views/Library/AlbumDetail/TrackRowView.swift`), the row as drawn from its values; `TrackRowView` there follows the pointer and the jump's flash for it.
