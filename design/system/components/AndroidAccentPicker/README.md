# AndroidAccentPicker

Under the Accent title, across the card: six swatches, Blue, Orchid, Pink, Lime, Mint and Sky.

The chosen colour is named on the Accent row's second line, in secondary text (“Pink”), as Theme and Tone show theirs: a swatch alone says nothing to someone who can't tell the colours apart. Each swatch's name is also what TalkBack reads. The swatches' group has no name of its own: the Accent row over it names it.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidAccentPicker` (`ui/settings/AppearanceSection.kt`).
