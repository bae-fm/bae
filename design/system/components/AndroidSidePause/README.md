# AndroidSidePause

The dialog when playback pauses at the end of a side or disc, as `MacSidePause`: the title names what ended (`kind` side or disc, `label` its name), then Playback is paused. Press Play to continue., the seconds until the next one starts (`countdown`), and the Pause between sides box, checked. `unchecked` clears the box and adds where to turn the pause back on. Close and Play end it.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidSidePause` (`ui/playback/AndroidNowPlayingBarContainer.kt`).
