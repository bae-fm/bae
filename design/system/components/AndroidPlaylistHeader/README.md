# AndroidPlaylistHeader

A playlist page's head: its picture (`covers`), its name over the owner, track count and who it is shared with (`facts`), then Play under them. The playlist's options are the ⋮ of the screen's top bar (`AndroidDetailScreen` with `menu` and `menuLabel` "Playlist options").

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidPlaylistHeader` (`ui/library/PlaylistScreen.kt`).
