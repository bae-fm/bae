# AndroidButton

`filled` for the action a screen is for, `outlined` beside a filled one, `tonal` for an action that stands alone without being what the screen is for (a centred notice's Retry or Finish resetting, New playlist), `text` in dialogs and rows. An outline only ever marks the lesser of two. `large` for the welcome screen's actions. A `filled` button is the accent's deep fill (`accent-selection`) with white text, the same as the Mac's default button and the keyboard's enter key. Beside a `destructive` text button, the other one (Cancel, Keep) is `neutral`, in the screen's text colour, so the accent and the danger colour never sit side by side. An `inverse` text button sits on the inverse surface, as a snackbar's Retry does, in `accent-inverse`, Material's inverse primary: the other theme's accent, which reads on the inverse surface in both themes. Words are a verb, sentence case, no ellipsis. `icon` puts a glyph before the words, as the album page's Pin and Unpin do. A `stage` button sits on the gallery's backdrop, which is dark in both modes: it draws in dark mode's colours, so a tonal button's fill is dark mode's tile under the backdrop's white words, and a text button takes dark mode's accent. `AndroidNotice` passes it to its buttons on the backdrop.

A `text` button on its own line lines its words up with the text above it: `alignTo: 'text'` under plain text, flush with it; `alignTo: 'field'` under a field, with the field's text, 16dp in.

`off` is Material's disabled button, the same for every kind and tone: the words in the screen's text at 38%, the fill of a filled or tonal one and an outline in it at 12%, so a disabled Join reads grey, not a faded accent.

**In the code:** Android: `AndroidButton` (`ui/components/AndroidButton.kt`), Material's filled, tonal, outlined and text buttons by `kind`, with `tone` destructive or neutral, `large`, and `alignTo` (text or field) for a text button on its own line. The album page's pin control (`AndroidPinControl` in `ui/albumdetail/AndroidPinControl.kt`) passes an outlined one its glyph as `icon`.
